The Holy Trinity

Understanding the Mystery of Three Persons in One God

What is the Trinity?

The Trinity is the central mystery of the Christian faith. It reveals that there is one God who exists in three distinct persons: the Father, the Son (Jesus Christ), and the Holy Spirit. These three persons are co-equal, co-eternal, and share the same divine essence.

Biblical Foundation

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."

— Matthew 28:19

The Trinity is revealed throughout Scripture, from the baptism of Jesus where all three persons are present, to the many references to the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit working together in salvation.

The Three Persons

God the Father

The first person of the Trinity, the source and origin of all creation.

God the Son

Jesus Christ, the second person who became incarnate for our salvation.

God the Holy Spirit

The third person who sanctifies and guides the Church and believers.

Living the Trinity

Understanding the Trinity helps us in our prayer life and relationship with God. We pray to the Father, through the Son, in the Holy Spirit. This mystery also teaches us about perfect love and unity in diversity.

The Seven Sacraments

Sacred Signs Instituted by Christ

What are Sacraments?

Sacraments are efficacious signs of grace, instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church, by which divine life is dispensed to us. They are visible signs of invisible grace.

The Seven Sacraments

Baptism

The gateway to the Christian life, washing away original sin.

Confirmation

Strengthens the grace of Baptism through the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

Eucharist

The source and summit of Christian life - the Body and Blood of Christ.

Reconciliation

Forgiveness of sins committed after Baptism through God's mercy.

Anointing of the Sick

Spiritual and sometimes physical healing for the seriously ill.

Holy Orders

Ordination of deacons, priests, and bishops to serve the Church.

Matrimony

The sacred union between a man and woman in Christ.

Categories of Sacraments

Sacraments of Initiation

Baptism, Confirmation, and Eucharist - the foundation of Christian life.

Sacraments of Healing

Reconciliation and Anointing of the Sick - God's mercy and healing.

Sacraments of Service

Holy Orders and Matrimony - serving God and community.

Essential Catholic Prayers

Fundamental Prayers Every Catholic Should Know

The Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

The Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee; blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

The Glory Be

Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

The Apostles' Creed

I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died and was buried; he descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.
