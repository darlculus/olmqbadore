Photo Upload Guidelines

What to Upload

  • Photos from parish events, masses, and celebrations
  • Community activities and fellowship moments
  • Sacramental ceremonies (baptisms, weddings, confirmations)
  • Youth ministry and children's activities
  • Construction and renovation progress
  • Harvest celebrations and special occasions

What Not to Upload

  • Blurry, dark, or poor quality images
  • Photos without proper consent from subjects
  • Images that don't relate to parish activities
  • Copyrighted material or stock photos
  • Inappropriate or offensive content

Technical Requirements

  • File formats: JPG, PNG, GIF
  • Maximum file size: 5MB per image
  • Minimum resolution: 800x600 pixels
  • Maximum of 10 photos per upload session

Privacy & Consent

  • Ensure you have permission to photograph people
  • Be mindful of children's privacy
  • Respect those who prefer not to be photographed
  • All uploads are reviewed before publication

Review Process

All uploaded photos go through a review process by our parish media team. This typically takes 1-3 business days. You'll receive an email notification once your photos are approved and published.
