Our Journey

The Story of OLMQ

From humble beginnings to a thriving parish community that has touched thousands of lives

1998

Foundation & Early Beginnings

Our Lady Mother and Queen Catholic Church was established as a mission station under the guidance of the Archdiocese of Lagos. What started as a small gathering of faithful families has grown into a vibrant community.

25 Founding Families
1 Visiting Priest
2001

First Permanent Structure

Construction of our first permanent church building was completed, providing a sacred space for 200 faithful. This marked a significant milestone in our parish's growth and stability.

200 Seating Capacity
₦2M Construction Cost
2005

Educational Ministry Launch

Established our comprehensive religious education program and launched the OLMQ Catholic School. This marked our commitment to holistic formation of young minds in faith and academics.

150 First Students
12 Teaching Staff
2015

Community Outreach Expansion

Launched our comprehensive social services program including medical outreach, skills acquisition center, and food bank. This expansion reflected our commitment to serving the whole person.

500+ Families Served Monthly
8 Outreach Programs
2024

Silver Jubilee Preparation

As we approach our 25th anniversary, we're preparing special celebrations and launching new community projects to mark this milestone in our parish journey.

1200+ Parish Families
18 Active Ministries
