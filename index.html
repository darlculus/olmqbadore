Our Mission

To build a vibrant Catholic community that lives out the Gospel message through worship, service, and fellowship, guided by the intercession of Our Lady Mother and Queen.

Our Vision

We members of Our Lady Mother & Queen Catholic Church, will foster the spiritual growth of our Parish through prayer, sacraments, devotions and communal understanding

Our Story

Established in 199, Our Lady Mother and Queen Catholic Church has been a cornerstone of faith in the Badore community. From humble beginnings to our current vibrant parish, we continue to grow in faith and service.

Christmas Pastoral Letter 2024

Archdiocese of Lagos

"Emmanuel: God With Us in Times of Challenge"

Most Rev. Alfred Adewale Martins December 16, 2024

Dear Brothers and Sisters in Christ,

As we enter this holy season of Christmas, I write to you with joy and hope, reflecting on the profound mystery of the Incarnation - God becoming man to dwell among us.

The name Emmanuel, meaning "God with us," reminds us that in our times of challenge, uncertainty, and even despair, we are not alone. Our Lord Jesus Christ, born in Bethlehem over two thousand years ago, continues to be present with us today...

Christmas Schedule:

  • Christmas Novena: December 16-24, 6:00 PM daily
  • Christmas Eve Mass: December 24, 11:30 PM
  • Christmas Day Masses: December 25, 7:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM

I encourage all parishes to organize special programs for the needy during this season, remembering that Christ came especially for the poor and marginalized...

Advent Reflections & Christmas Preparations

Rev. Fr. Leo Garber - Parish Priest

December 15, 2024

Dear beloved parishioners,

As we journey through this holy season of Advent, I invite you to prepare your hearts for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. This Christmas, let us focus on the true meaning of Emmanuel - God with us.

In these times of global challenges and personal struggles, the message of Christmas brings us hope and comfort. God chose to become one of us, to share in our humanity, and to show us the way to eternal life.

Parish Christmas Activities:

  • Daily Advent Reflections after evening Mass
  • Christmas decoration of the church - volunteers needed
  • Children's Christmas program rehearsals every Saturday
  • Christmas hamper distribution for needy families

I encourage each family to create a special time for prayer and reflection during this Advent season. Let us prepare not just our homes, but our hearts for the Prince of Peace.

May this Christmas bring you and your families abundant blessings, peace, and joy.

In Christ,
Rev. Fr. [Name]
